How a Critical Hit is Calculated

The standard hit (39.6 s) is counted ONCE, whatever the number of rolls.

Each landed roll then adds the crit bonus: 50% base + 50% Chopper + 10% per level

Perk lv. 1: 39.6 s + 1 × 43.56 s = 83.16 s  ·  2 rolls: 39.6 + 2 × 43.56 = 126.72 s
