🎵 Your sounds
Any mp3, wav, ogg or m4a up to 5 MB. The app keeps its own copy and matches its loudness to the other alerts. Once added, pick it for any timer above.
Best length: about 1–5 seconds. Longer clips are faded out at 8 seconds, and short ones don't pile up when two timers go off close together (Kyber at :15 and a Mission can land within seconds). A 5-second mp3 is usually well under 1 MB; if your file is bigger than 5 MB, trim it to the part you want first.
